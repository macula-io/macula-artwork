## The rules, as NVIDIA publishes them

Source: https://design.nvidia.com/partners/inception/nvidia-inception-program/member-badge#usage
(read 2026-09-24). Quoted text is verbatim; the two measurements are drawn in
the page's illustrations, not written out, and are described here as they appear.

**Colour variants.** "The NVIDIA Inception program badge works on both light and
dark backgrounds." "The colors built into the badge must not be changed or
adjusted." The page shows the full-colour badge (white panel, black border) for
light backgrounds and the same full-colour badge on a dark background, and the
single-colour badge for single-colour output. "Note: Single-color logos are
available but should only be used when full-color reproduction isn't possible."

**Colours.** "Digital applications use RGB or HEX color values." NVIDIA Green
`#76b900` (RGB 118, 185, 0), Black `#000000`, White `#ffffff`. "Colors within
the artwork files shouldn't be changed or adjusted."

**Clear space.** "Ensure a minimum clear space so the design doesn't appear
cluttered." The illustration measures it as one height of the NVIDIA eye mark
on every side of the badge, including between the badge and the partner's own
logo. In these files the eye mark is 68 of the badge panel's 166 units, so the
clear space is about 0.41 × the panel's height. The SVG's own transparent
margin (about 25 units) counts toward it but is not enough on its own.

**Minimum size.** "It is important to present the NVIDIA logo as large and bold
as possible. When faced with space limitations, these are the minimum sizing
requirements so that the logo is legible." Digital: 30px (the badge's height).
Print: 0.25in / 6.5mm.

**Do nots.** "The following examples of how not to use NVIDIA Inception branding
applies to all logos and text lockups."

- "Do not change the badge color."
- "Do not change the proportions of the logo."
- "Do not alter or replace the text in the badge."
- "Do not make the badge bigger than the partner logo."

**Questions.** "All other partners, please reach out to your NVIDIA account
representative for support."

## How Macula uses it

On macula.io, in the site footer: the full-colour SVG, unmodified, at least 30px
tall, with at least one eye-mark height of clear space, smaller than the Macula
logo on the same page, linking to the program page, beside the text "Member of
NVIDIA Inception Program". The portal serves its own copy of the SVG; nothing
hotlinks here.
